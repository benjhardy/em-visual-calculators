Visual calculators

RF and microwave · open source

Calculators that show the physics

Every input redraws the fields or currents along with the number. Every page shows its method and checks itself against published values.

01 · Conductors

Skin effect

Where AC current flows in a wire. Exact Bessel solution, Rac/Rdc, flat-conductor view.

checked: R/R0 table, ber/bei series
02 · Transmission lines

Coaxial line

Z0, loss, TE11 cutoff (exact root) and peak power. Why 30, 60 and 77 Ω.

checked: cable data, optima, TE11 roots
03 · Planar lines

Microstrip, solved live

A finite-difference Laplace solve in your browser, next to Hammerstad–Jensen. Synthesis and dispersion.

checked: 20-case sweep vs H–J, < 0.4%
04 · Waveguides

Rectangular waveguide

TE and TM patterns, the mode chart, λg, vp, vg, impedance, loss and evanescent decay.

checked: Pozar Example 3.1
05 · Propagation

Path loss and link budget

Spherical spreading and effective aperture, Friis, rain (ITU-R P.838-3) and a waterfall budget.

checked: P.525 values, P.838-3 table
